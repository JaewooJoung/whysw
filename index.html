What is Blunux?

  • Arch Linux that anyone can use
  • KDE Plasma desktop out of the box
  • Installs in minutes, rolling updates
  • MIT licensed — free forever
🔴 Open it live
After the reveal, tell your story: what annoyed you about installing/using Linux? How long until the first working ISO? Then open the live site for ~30 seconds. Ask: "Who here has tried Linux at home?" (~4 min)
🐟 2 / 3 · iOS app

Aktiesignal — what does it trade with?

  • Real Stockholm exchange data, 10 000 kr of pretend money
  • Daily signal: Strong Buy → Strong Sell (RSI, MACD, Bollinger…)
  • Names hidden behind numbers — learn the method, not the hype
  • Hit rate always shown with its sample size
🔴 App Store

Scan it now — install during the talk 📱

Story: why anonymise the stocks? Why insist on showing sample size next to hit rate? Ask: "Would you trust a 90% hit rate from 3 trades?" Not investment advice 😉 (~4 min)
🐟 3 / 3 · blunod.se

Who is blunod built for?

  • A map of the places that matter to truck drivers
  • Weigh stations · border crossings · port gates
  • Works offline — where coverage dies, it doesn't
🔴 Open it live
This one is closest to our daily work — trucks. Story: where did the idea come from? Ask the team: "What other spots would a driver want on this map?" (~4 min)
